## lastmod review

`lib/seo.ts:24-46` deliberately stores content dates, not build times; `README.md:39-55` documents updating them for significant content, links or structured data changes. Commit `21bf86e` (2026-10-06) introduced the metadata definitions and changed all three public pages plus shared catalog metadata. That supports the common date.

The later relevant page commit `84f8f5f` (2026-10-14) changes only Photo Graph image URL construction from Supabase rendering to provider-aware delivery. This is media-provider plumbing, not demonstrated changed editorial content or a reason by itself to stamp all pages October 14/15. No subsequent committed change to the reviewed public-page/catalog/identity paths establishes a later significant content date. A sitemap HTTP `Last-Modified` header/cache timestamp is likewise not the individual pages' content-update date.

**Minimal action:** retain these dates unless comparison with actual published content or a future significant edit proves a newer page-specific date. On such an edit, update only the corresponding `PUBLIC_PAGES.*.lastModified`; verify sitemap output and that page's canonical after the separately authorized release. Do not automatically refresh dates for deployments, object copies, storage-provider migrations or every upload.

The date review cannot prove every historical production deployment or mutable backend change; no later significant change is established by the inspected evidence. Sitemap validity and crawlability do not prove Google indexing or Google's selected canonical. No submissions were performed.
