# Dexter Young portfolio

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Present Dexter Young's software and multimedia projects, let visitors explore
the work, and explain the process behind each project through write-ups.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Project identity and links come from `app/components/projects/project-catalog.ts`.
- The homepage About section pairs a short introduction with a scrollable list
  of every catalogue project that has a write-up, using the contact-link treatment.
- Write-ups are plain articles with accompanying static media and an interactive
  project preview at the top. Full project experiences retain their own URLs.
- Photo Graph's existing write-up URL and interactive experience remain distinct.
- Elliot's completed write-up describes the minimal interface and colour-based
  navigation, illustrated with screenshots of the public site.
- bur1alrites has a write-up about Allie's reactive lighting, the 3D gallery,
  and the projector room. Its motivation and design decisions came from Dexter.
- Photo Graph's review and result images are responsive galleries of individual
  local photographs, with labels and ranked model rows rendered as HTML.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the portfolio's existing monochrome, editorial, square-edged identity,
as described in `AGENTS.md`. Project imagery supplies colour; the surrounding
interface stays restrained.

## Evidence on Hand

Photo Graph has an existing project poster and a dated colour-model benchmark
under `public/projects/`. Benchmark findings describe that saved experiment,
not a live count of the archive. Elliot's design rationale was supplied by Dexter;
its public-site screenshots have a source manifest under `public/projects/`.
The bur1alrites article has the same public-capture provenance, and Photo Graph's
individual gallery images retain their source checksums and benchmark ordering.
Do not invent client feedback, process claims, or measured results.
